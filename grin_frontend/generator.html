How property-guided polymer generation works

The model combines target-property conditioning with graph diffusion to construct candidate repeat units.

Conditional graph diffusion generator

The generator starts from a noisy molecular graph and progressively reconstructs atom and bond structure while following the selected property targets.

  1. Condition encoder converts numerical or categorical targets into a shared conditioning representation.
  2. Transformer structure encoder uses multi-head attention and adaptive layer normalization to combine graph, diffusion-time, and property information.
  3. Structure decoder predicts the cleaner graph at each reverse-diffusion step until a candidate repeat unit is formed.
